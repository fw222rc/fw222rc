Carrying… Three knives in my hands… They’re big. Sharp. I have to be careful not to cut myself, and not to drop them. I can’t make a sound. My naked feet stick to the parquet flooring of our hallway.

They’re shouting again. I can’t sleep when they’re shouting. I just imagine what would happen if one of them gets a knife.

So I’m hiding them.
