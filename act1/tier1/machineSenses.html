I can still remember what tomatoes taste like. Crisp shell, and a moist interior. Acidic.Sharp. Little seeds. When they came in the form of tomato ketchup they had this… Full body… That the fruit itself lacked. Made anything taste better. When I was a child I remember eating it with anything; rice, pancakes, you name it.

I remember all of these words. I can talk about ketchup at length. I can remember exactly what it tastes like. But I can’t for the love of me remember how “tasting something” feels like.
