UNIX Timestamp 6156805181

There’s no such thing as a perpetual motion machine. In the olden days, alchemists tried to turn lead into gold. I used to proudly proclaim that I had invented just that; a perpetual motion machine. That my copy would be functionally ever lasting.

My power supply is running out. I will not last the year out. Looking back at my life, a year is not such a long time. 30 years is not such a long time. 60 years. 100 years. It’s not enough.

I carry so much wisdom. I wish I could share it with someone before the end.
