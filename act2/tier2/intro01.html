Recorded in 2032

Do I look proud? Do I look like a million bucks? Probably more right, because as of today all of Sweden will start rolling my trains underground. 40 minutes from Kiruna to Malmö. That’s ten times faster than SJ.

And we’re already in talks with Denmark to get our own track over Öresund.

This is going so fast. It was just a couple of years since I sold computers from my moms basement.
